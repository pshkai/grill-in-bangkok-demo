## Profile and uncertainties

Grill In Bangkok is an Asian BBQ/Chinese restaurant in Ekkamai–Thonglor, Bangkok, serving grilled dishes, skewers and shared plates. Likely audience, inferred from menu and dining listings: groups of friends, families and local/international diners. Primary official communication language cannot be established confidently; matching posts use English alongside Thai tags and platform content. Website supports both languages; English outreach is chosen provisionally because of English promotional copy, not claimed as a confirmed primary language.

No authenticated visual identity, independent official domain, authorized photo set, confirmed schedule, current full à-la-carte prices, accessibility details, parking terms or allergy information was obtained. No assumptions replace these gaps. Published booking platforms are real channels but not guaranteed to have tables.

## Assessment (design inferences)

Strengths: a distinctive spicy/grilled menu; a recognizable mall location; established social handles; bookable dining-platform listings.

Three opportunities visible in this research: business information is fragmented across platforms; inconsistent contact/hours can create uncertainty; menu and visit planning require switching contexts. Direct social access was restricted, so this is not a claim that the original mobile navigation or visual quality is poor.

Customer benefits: a bilingual, searchable-by-category menu overview; one place for location, verified landline and real booking links; a mobile-first visit planner that makes the distinction between a preview and actual booking explicit.

Owner proposition: a tailored, maintainable static website bringing discovery and contact actions into a coherent experience, with a future approved reservation integration. No conversion or sales promises.

## Design strategy

Warm charcoal, terracotta and parchment with editorial serif headlines and practical sans-serif controls. This is a proposed concept palette, not a verified official brand treatment. A text identity avoids inventing an official logo. The skewers illustration, Thonglor placement, numbered menu and group-dining structure make the experience specific to this restaurant. Motion is limited to hero entrance and brief hover feedback and respects reduced motion. No competitor copied.
