What still needs confirmation

Opening hours conflict: Wongnai lists noon–midnight, Chope noon–1am, Tripadvisor 2pm–midnight, and a matching Lemon8 profile publishes later hours. Please contact the restaurant before travelling. Restaurant Guru lists a different mobile number, so we use the landline corroborated by Wongnai and Tripadvisor.

A matching Lemon8 profile publishes a LINE short link, but the link opens LINE account @956zgaza without identifying the business. Account ownership remains unverified. LINE integration is pending. The Maps-listed website, grillinbangkok.com, failed DNS resolution during inspection. Social account language could not be reliably established; the website offers both English and Thai.

Table grilling descriptions also differ across listings and diner reports. The concept does not promise a particular grill setup. Current prices, accessibility, parking terms, allergens and availability need direct confirmation. No customer rating, testimonial or review quotation is reproduced.

Artwork & privacy

The hero is AI-generated concept artwork made with OpenAI ImageGen. It does not show actual restaurant food or premises. The text identity and graphic poster are original demo design. No business photographs or official logo have been reused.

The visit planner is entirely browser-local. It collects no name, email or telephone number, saves nothing, sends nothing and checks no live availability. The result explicitly states that no reservation has been submitted. Real bookings require contacting the restaurant or using the linked dining platforms. Google Fonts supplies the free DM Sans and Noto Sans Thai fonts; system fonts are used if unavailable.
